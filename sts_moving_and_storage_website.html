Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in your White Glove Delivery service?

Our White Glove Delivery service includes inside placement in the room of your choice, full unboxing, precision furniture assembly, and complete removal of all packing materials and debris.

How do you support interior designers and architects?

We offer dedicated receiving, itemized inventory inspection, climate-controlled warehousing, consolidation, and scheduled white-glove installation to complete design projects effortlessly.

Are my items fully insured during moving and storage?

Yes! STS Moving and Storage is fully licensed, bonded, and insured. We prioritize safety and offer comprehensive liability coverage options for your valuable property.

What is the difference between Threshold and White Glove delivery?

Threshold delivery safely places your items inside the front entryway or garage of your home. White glove delivery includes in-room placement, full setup, and packing cleanup.

How can I schedule a moving estimate or get a free quote?

You can fill out our online quote form below, call us directly at (850) 257-7059, or click our Google My Business quote link to request a tailored estimate within hours.

What hours are you available for client support?

Our business hours are Monday through Friday from 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM. We also arrange weekend specialized deliveries by prior appointment.
